Local-first policy · 28 August 2026

Privacy

Frame UV Lab does not upload your sprite sheets, frame maps, or effect settings. They remain in your browser.

Data on your device

The current workspace is stored in IndexedDB so it can survive refreshes and work offline. A Pro license token and a daily verification result are stored in localStorage. You can remove this data through your browser’s site-data controls.

Network requests

The installed app checks its own files for updates. If you buy or verify Pro, your browser contacts api.sociobot.in. The checkout provider receives information needed to complete your purchase under its own policy. The free editor makes no analytics or tracking requests.

Your art

Image decoding, frame selection, previews, and exports happen on this device. Exported files are saved only when you ask. We cannot see or recover your workspace.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the operator through sociobot.in.
